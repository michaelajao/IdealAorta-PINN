Experiment revision required before restart (not implemented or sealed)
The primary question is whether the manuscript's neural-versus-RBF ranking
survives controlled noise and averaging with matched information. A new
divergence-free comparator alone cannot answer that question.

The revised design must include the prespecified historical RBF, its space-time
RBF sensitivity, data-only and continuity neural fields, and the proposed scalar
and divergence-free fits where feasible. Volume conditions additionally need
a naive baseline that treats an average as a point observation, compared with
the same model using the correct averaging operator. Freeze fitting/validation
rows, boundary information, no-refit policy and hidden scoring targets.

Separate temporal treatment from representation: report velocity and WSS
comparisons alongside acceleration and pressure, and include a temporal RBF
comparison before attributing pressure differences to the spatial architecture.
Independent snapshot fits can amplify independent observation noise in finite
differences. This is a design confound to test, not a proven explanation.

Expand support/ridge grids when selection is on their boundary, with declared
resource limits and observation-only tuning. Capacity coverage must include
noise-free conditions and matched near-wall/random-removal controls, with a
declared draw policy. Numerical-solve accuracy needs an error bound or a tighter
reference-solve sensitivity that is small relative to the 2% capacity decision;
an optimizer residual alone cannot certify that prediction accuracy.

Use separate boundary diagnostics by aneurysm zone/neck and other wall regions,
including upper quantiles/maxima and WSS-region slip. Selecting candidates using
the same global boundary criterion does not independently validate no-slip
accuracy; a lack of admissible candidates must be retained as a failure.

Full-box interior averaging excludes crossing-wall boxes and does not simulate
partial volume. Either add an explicit, physically stated wall-crossing forward
model with independent verification, or restrict the claim to interior averaging
and leave MRI/WSS realism unresolved. Do not silently equate these designs.

Record a revised method/condition matrix and cost estimate before scheduling
new work. The old 128/384 counts cannot describe an expanded method matrix.
Do not resume either old controller automatically or mutate its code snapshot.
